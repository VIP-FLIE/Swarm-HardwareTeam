# Output Standard (Research / Digital Deliverables)

Every research deliverable follows the same shape so it can be read, trusted, and reused after its author has graduated. Start from [templates/research-output.md](../templates/research-output.md).

## Location and naming

`research/<topic>/<slug>.md`

- `<topic>` is a broad area, e.g. `components`, `power`, `px4`, `positioning`, `interface`.
- `<slug>` is short and specific, e.g. `motors-escs`.

## Suggested sections

1. **Question**: what this document is trying to answer.
2. **Short answer**: the conclusion in a few sentences. A reader who stops here should still get the point.
3. **Findings**: the supporting detail, organized so it can be skimmed.
4. **Sources**: link, title, and date accessed for each. Prefer primary sources (datasheets, official docs) over summaries.
5. **Confidence and gaps**: what you're sure of, what you're not, and what you couldn't find. Label unverified claims explicitly.
6. **Next actions**: follow-up tasks this suggests. Turn them into ClickUp tasks; don't leave them only in the file.
7. **Links**: the ClickUp task, and any related repo documents.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Question and Short answer are both filled in.
- [ ] Every factual claim has a source, or is labeled unverified.
- [ ] Confidence and gaps section is honest and non-empty.
- [ ] Follow-up work exists as ClickUp tasks.
- [ ] Links to its ClickUp task, and the ClickUp task links to it.
- [ ] Peer reviewer has approved in ClickUp.
